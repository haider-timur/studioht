Haider Timur (he/him) is a graphic designer and hybrid media artist based in Amsterdam. 

As the founder of Studio Haider Timur, he began a sound, performance, and installation practice to create narrative-driven performances and express his artistic vision.  

Haider’s work has been exhibited and screened at Framer Framed (Amsterdam, NL), Page Not Found (The Hague, NL), No Limits! Art Castle (Amsterdam, NL), Kunstfort Vijfhuizen (NL), Keizersgrachtkerk (Amsterdam, NL), National Museum of Astana (KZ), The Grey Space in the Middle (The Hague, NL), Bult (Almaty, KZ), Radion (Amsterdam, NL).

Including talks and interviews featured in Mr. Motley (NL), Babalar Press (KZ), Red Light Radio (Amsterdam, NL), Amsterdam Museum (NL), LAB111 (Amsterdam, NL) among others.
