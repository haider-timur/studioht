<p>Haider is a member of an experimental music duo called 'buulbuul' together with artist Kassym Kushkimbayev. He has also supported Eirini Kakopierou with sound design and voice recordings for performances at OCCII (Amsterdam, NL) and Van Gogh Museum (Amsterdam, NL). His other collaborative work is participating in the Alternative Typographic Histories course at the School of Poetic Computation/SFPC (NYC).</p>
